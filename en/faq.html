Which book should I open first?

These are not mushaf pages. Each page is one chapter of a book.

  • The short start, 11 chapters: Ibn Taymiyyah, Muqaddima.

  • Al-Suyuti, 96 chapters: al-Itqan.

What is t3.mushafuk.online?

This address holds classical books on Quranic sciences and the method of tafsir. Each chapter is its own page.

How many books are here?

There are 7 books: the principles of tafsir, the sciences of the Quran, the occasions of revelation, rare words, and the virtues of the Quran.

How many pages are here?

The seven books have 726 reading pages. These are chapters of the works, not Quran pages.

The books and their pages

The figure is the number of chapters in that book.

  • al-Burhan, al-Zarkashi — 194

How do I open a page?
